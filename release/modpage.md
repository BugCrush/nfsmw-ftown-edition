# NFSMW F-Town Edition

**A new map, a new car and the Xbox 360 look for Need for Speed Most Wanted (2005), in one installer.**

## What you get

- **F-Town**, a new map built from OpenStreetMap: pick it from Quick Race > Bonus Maps. Free Roam, Time Attack and
  three events (a drag, a speed trap, a sector sprint), traffic on every road, an evening version, and a road with a
  jump that joins it to Rockport.
- **The Mahindra Thar**, made for this mod from a CC-BY model, plus five add-on cars by other authors: the 1971 Dodge
  Challenger, the Fast & Furious Flip Car, the Bugatti Chiron, the Ford F-150 SVT Raptor and a Longnose Cab semi truck.
- **The Xbox 360 look**: the Xbox 360 Stuff Pack with its cutscenes, music and engine sounds, 1024x vinyls and ReTex
  2.02 textures, with our picture effects on top: ambient occlusion, light shafts, distance haze, wet roads, edge
  smoothing, world shadows.
- **Controller and music**: separate triggers and rumble for Xbox controllers; your own MP3s in the game's playlist.

## How it installs

Our download holds only our own work. You download the other authors' mods from their pages (the list and links are
in the README), put them in one folder, and run our installer once. It checks every file against the exact version it
was tested with, backs up every game file before changing it, and its uninstall puts the game back byte for byte.
A missing download skips only the parts that need it.

## Requirements

- NFS Most Wanted (2005) PC, version 1.3, Black Edition files, unmodded (the installer checks; other versions are
  refused until tested). English game text.
- About 7 GB free on the game's drive (2 GB of it is the uninstall backup); Windows 10/11; a Vulkan graphics card.
- Tested on a GTX 1650 Ti laptop: about 65 to 95 FPS in races at 1920x1080. The README says how to turn the
  heaviest effects off on slower machines.

## Notes

- Some antivirus programs distrust installers made from Python programs. The installer's source is public, and every
  file in the download is listed with its SHA-256 in `payload-manifest.json`.
- F-Town has no race opponents yet (Time Attack and the events are against the clock).
